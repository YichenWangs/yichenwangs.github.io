I'm Yichen (逸宸), a postdoctoral research fellow in the France 2030 [PEPR eNSEMBLE](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/en/) program. I work at the intersection of musical creativity and computer science, hosted by the [MINT team](https://mint.univ-lille.fr) at the Université de Lille.
My research encompasses [new interfaces for musical expression](https://www.nime.org), augmented reality, and the entangled nature of digital music ensembles in Human-Computer Interaction. 
I completed my PhD in computer science at the [SMCClab](https://smcclab.au/), The Australian National University.


My [research works](works) include the intelligent instrument *溯*, the mixed reality musical instrument *cube*, and the networked augmented reality collaborative expression system *arMIDI*, with [creative practices](performances) along the way.
