I'm Yichen (逸宸), an interaction designer and HCI researcher with a PhD in Computer Science and industry R&D experience. I experiment with emerging technologies across mixed reality, audio, and AI, connecting ideas from design, sound, and computing. My work combines concept development, hands-on prototyping with Unity, JavaScript, and audio programming, and evaluation through user studies and musical performance.

## [+] Selected projects

- **[Sù 溯]({{ '/su' | relative_url }})** — A real-time generative AI musical interface with direct latent-domain controls for creative sound exploration.
- **[arMIDI]({{ '/collab-ar' | relative_url }})** — A collaborative augmented reality (AR) interface with shared visualisations of musicians’ gestures and instrument activity to support mutual awareness during electronic music performance.
- **[cube]({{ '/cube' | relative_url }})** — An AR musical instrument with freehand gestural interaction and spatially arranged tonal cubes for expressive sound creation through movement.
- **[off-the-shelf]({{ '/embodied-ai-ar' | relative_url }})** — A human–AI musical collaboration system with AR visualisations of an AI agent’s musical activity to support awareness and engagement during shared performance.

## [<] Background

I'm currently a research fellow in the France 2030 [PEPR eNSEMBLE](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/en/) program, hosted by the [MINT team](https://mint.univ-lille.fr) at the Université de Lille. I develop interaction concepts that make AI behaviour more understandable during creative collaboration, and investigate how AI tools influence creative agency and roles.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at [The Australian National University](https://smcclab.au/) in 2025, researching spatial musical interface design with augmented reality.
