I'm Yichen (逸宸), an interaction designer and HCI researcher with a PhD in Computer Science and industry R&D experience. I experiment with emerging technologies across mixed reality, audio, and AI, connecting ideas from design, sound, and computing. My work combines concept development, hands-on prototyping with Unity, JavaScript, and audio programming, and evaluation through user studies and musical performance.

## [+] Selected projects

- **[Sù 溯]({{ '/su' | relative_url }})** — An AI-assisted musical interface translating a complex latent synthesis model into direct creative controls for real-time generative audio and spatialisation.
- **[arMIDI]({{ '/adc-poster/collab-ar' | relative_url }})** — An AR interface for collaborative performance. Evaluation with four musician pairs explored how visual feedback supports shared awareness and competes for attention.
- **[cube]({{ '/adc-poster/cube' | relative_url }})** — Three gesture-based AR interaction concepts, developed through iterative self-testing and evaluated with 20 users to explore learnability, creative exploration, and sound customisation.
- **[off-the-shelf]({{ '/adc-poster/embodied-ai-ar' | relative_url }})** — An AR interface for human-AI musical collaboration, explored through performances and a study with 16 users in solo and duo groups.

Explore [all projects]({{ '/works' | relative_url }}) and [performances]({{ '/performances' | relative_url }}), or [get in touch](mailto:hallo@yichenwang.io).

## [<] Background

I'm currently a research fellow in the France 2030 [PEPR eNSEMBLE](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/en/) program, hosted by the [MINT team](https://mint.univ-lille.fr) at the Université de Lille. I develop interaction concepts that make AI behaviour more understandable during creative collaboration, and investigate how AI tools influence creative agency and roles.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at [The Australian National University](https://smcclab.au/) in 2025, researching spatial musical interface design with augmented reality.
